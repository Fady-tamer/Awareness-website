Earth’s Vital Signs & Climate Overview.

Human activities have systematically altered global temperature records, driven extreme weather patterns, and shifted biological habitats worldwide.

Explore Effects

Main Human Causes

  • Burning Fossil Fuels

    The combustion of coal, oil, and natural gas for electricity, heat, transportation, and industrial manufacturing releases massive amounts of carbon dioxide (CO₂) into the atmosphere.

  • Deforestation and Land-Use Changes

    Clearing forests for agriculture, urbanization, or logging removes natural carbon sinks that absorb CO₂ from the air. When trees are cut down or burned, stored carbon is released back into the atmosphere.

    • Agriculture and Livestock Farming

    • Methane (CH₄)

      Livestock such as cattle produce large quantities of methane during digestion. Organic waste in landfills also breaks down anaerobically to emit methane.

    • Nitrous Oxide (N₂O)

      Synthetic and organic agricultural fertilizers release potent nitrous oxide emissions into the atmosphere.

  • Industrial Processes

    Manufacturing, cement production, and the use of chemical products (such as fluorinated gases) release specialized heat-trapping emissions.

Natural Factors

  • Natural Variability

    Earth's climate has historically shifted due to natural cycles like volcanic eruptions, solar activity variations, and orbital shifts.

  • Role in Current Warming

    Natural factors alone cannot explain the rapid rate of modern warming; human emissions outpace natural CO₂ contributions by a factor of over 100 annually.
